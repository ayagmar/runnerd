runnerd reads its [job history](../../concepts/terms/#job-history) for what changed in your CI. [Notifications](../notifications/) send them to a phone or a webhook.

## Insights

A job is its repository, workflow and job name. Notifications send warnings, not notices.

| Insight | When |
|---|---|
| `slower` | The median of a job's last 5 successful runs on `main` or `master` is 20% and 30 s or more above the median of the runs before, and each of the 5 is slower than it. |
| `flaky` | A job failed or timed out, then passed on a re-run. Once is a notice, twice a warning. |
| `near-cap` | The p95 of a job's memory peaks over its last 10 runs is 85% or more of its profile's `memory`. A notice when [auto sizing](../sizing/#auto-sizing) raises it, else a warning. |
| `cold-cache` | At least 3 of a job's last 5 runs that could write to the [build cache](../caches/#build-cache) looked up 10 or more entries and found 10% or less. A notice. |
| `throttled` | The kernel throttled the downloads (`sock_throttled`) of 2 or more of a repository's jobs. A warning. |
| `cpu-held` | At least 3 of a job's last 10 successful runs were throttled by the CPU quota in 15% of their run or more. A notice while auto sizing raises `cpus`, else a warning. |
| `host-held` | A queued job has waited 30 minutes or more for the [host's memory](../../concepts/admission/#the-hosts-memory) while the host lacks room for a job that waits now. A warning, with the longest wait and the programs outside CI that hold the most memory; closing them frees only part of that figure. Sent at most once in each 6-hour window. |
| `host-low` | On a day CI ran, the host had under a tenth of its memory available while a [host credit](../../concepts/growth-and-priority/#the-growth-count) stood. A warning, at most once a day, naming `pooled_count = false` as the way back. |
| `waits` | A repository's p95 wait this week is half again and a minute more than last week's. A notice ([how a wait is counted](../sizing/#how-a-wait-is-counted)). |

`--days N` sets the window (default 14, at most 90); `--digest` shows the last 24 hours.

### The host's memory while CI ran

Below the insights, `runnerctl insights` and the dashboard list each UTC day a runner ran, and the digest gives its 24 hours, from the readings runnerd stored:

```text
host memory while CI ran:
  Nov 6: CI ran 9h 12m, 6h 10m of it on a host credit; under 19.4G available for 1h 04m (41m on a credit); under 6.2G available for 34s (34s on a credit); lowest 4.1G (4.5G on a credit); swap free at least 8.2G
```

The lines are the host's margin plus a quarter of its memory, and a tenth of its memory: 19.4G and 6.2G on a 62G host. Each reading counts until the next for a minute at most, so time runnerd was down is not counted.

## What the minutes would have cost

`runnerctl insights` opens with what this machine's job minutes would have cost on GitHub-hosted runners, this month (UTC) and over the last 30 days. Each job is billed as GitHub bills it: minutes rounded up, at the rate of the runner its profile's [`priced_as`](../../reference/configuration/#profilesname) names. Not counted:

- Jobs of public repositories on a standard runner, which GitHub runs free, and of repositories whose visibility is unknown.
- Jobs named `Dependabot Updates` or `pages build and deployment`, which GitHub runs free; a name is not proof, so they are neither priced nor called free.
- Lost jobs and jobs without a start or end.

The figure is before your plan's included minutes. When pruning has dropped jobs a window covers, the line says since when the history is complete.
