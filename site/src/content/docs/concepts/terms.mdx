## Slot

One concurrent job of a profile. Each job gets a new container; the slot keeps a volume over the profile's read-only snapshot, so the next job in it finds the Docker images, `~/.cache` and tool cache the last one left. A queued job starts in the free slot that last ran the same job (repository, workflow, job name), else one that ran a job of its repository, else the lowest free slot. [Caches](../../guides/caches/#warm-caches) says what a slot keeps.

## Profile and pool

A profile is a runner size and image: `cpus`, `memory`, `slots` and `image` under `[profiles.<name>]`. A pool is one `[[repositories]]` entry: a repository, the profile its jobs run on, and the labels it serves. A repository can have several pools, one per profile ([Split light and heavy jobs](../../guides/sizing/#split-light-and-heavy-jobs)).

## Slice and budget

Every runner container runs in one systemd slice, `runnerd.slice` as `runnerd init` writes it. Its `MemoryMax` is the budget: a runner starts only when it fits beside what already runs, and what [the host's memory](../admission/#the-hosts-memory) leaves the slice. A runner reserves its job's predicted peak ([measured admission](../admission/#measured-admission), the default), or its profile's `memory` while the job has too little history and always under `admission = "caps"`. <Budget />

`runnerd check-config [file]` reads the slice's `MemoryMax` from systemd, or takes `--memory-max`. Under measured admission it fails when one runner of a profile with the mirror does not fit, and refuses a slice without a `MemoryMax`. Under caps it fails when `slots × memory` over all profiles plus the mirror does not fit, and only warns about a slice without one:

```text
$ runnerd check-config runnerd.toml
runnerd.toml: valid under measured admission: the largest runner with the mirror needs 9.5G of the 33G MemoryMax; every slot at its cap at once would take 32.5G
$ runnerd check-config runnerd.toml --memory-max 9G
error: one runner of jvm and the mirror take 9.5G, the slice runnerd.slice's MemoryMax is 9G; lower its `memory` or raise the slice's MemoryMax
$ runnerd check-config caps.toml --memory-max 24G
error: slots × memory over all profiles plus the mirror is 32.5G, the slice runnerd.slice's MemoryMax is 24G; lower `slots` or `memory`, or raise the slice's MemoryMax (`runnerd doctor` prints its unit)
```

## Warm runner

A warm runner is registered before GitHub queues the job and takes it within a second or two, where a job that finds no runner waits for a new one to start ([how long](../../guides/waiting/#how-long-it-waited)).

- Each pool keeps [`warm`](../../reference/configuration/#repositories) runners (1 by default) while its repository is active: one of its jobs was waiting, taken or finished in the last 10 minutes. Otherwise they go after 5 idle minutes.
- When a job is seen, the other jobs of its run get runners too: per pool, the median number that workflow's runs had there over 14 days, a run with none there counting as 0, less those seen. A pool needs 3 runs with jobs there first, and one that most runs skip gets none.
- Warm runners start last, once every queued job that fits has a runner, and reserve their profile's `memory` (and `cpus` under CPU admission). A queued job of another repository takes their room. After a job in slot 1, the profile measures what slot 1 changed without stopping anything, at most every 10 minutes. Only a reseed due by that measure (slot 1 reached [`reseed_at`](../../reference/configuration-more/#caches)) or asked for removes them, once its other runners are idle.

## Record

With `record_failures = true` on a profile, a failed job's stopped runner is kept as an image, and `runnerctl shell <job>` opens a shell in it at the failing step. [Records of failed jobs](../../guides/records/) says what it keeps and who can read it.

## Attention

What needs you now, most urgent first, each with what clears it: queued jobs runnerd cannot start with the config it runs with (a paused repository, or labels no pool serves), a job that waited 30 minutes or more for the host's memory, the doctor's warnings and failures (or that its checks are not current), warning [insights](../../guides/insights/), a repository GitHub will not list, a token GitHub refuses, and a spent rate limit. The dashboard's overview and the TUI's open on it; `GET /v1/attention` serves it.

## Job history

runnerd records every job its runners may run in SQLite; [`[history]`](../../reference/configuration-more/#history) sets its path and retention. For each job:

| What | Kept |
|---|---|
| its timeline (queued, container started, job started, finished) and steps, as GitHub shows them | with the job |
| the container's CPU, memory and network every 2 s, its CPU throttling and the bytes the Docker Hub mirror served it | samples for `history.samples`; totals and peaks with the job |
| its log, secrets still masked, up to `history.log_max` | for `history.logs` |

A job's wait runs from queued to started; the part that passed while runnerd was not running is kept as `unseen` and left out of the waits [sizing](../../guides/sizing/#how-a-wait-is-counted) reads.

A job that leaves GitHub's queue without one of this machine's runners is recorded as `elsewhere` (the dashboard's "Not run here"), as `cancelled in queue` when its run was cancelled first, or as its conclusion, such as `skipped`. No wait, median or cost counts it. A finished job goes after `history.jobs`.

A failed job reads `runner fault` when Sysbox, not its code, failed it; [Why did this job fail?](../../guides/logs/#everything-else-on-its-page) says how runnerd tells.
